📖 How to read cameras:

Clear — road visible, few/no vehicles queuing
Moderate — steady flow, some queuing visible
Heavy — bumper-to-bumper, vehicles not moving

🗺 Camera Guide — What to look for

📷
#2701 — Causeway (Key Camera)
Shows the actual checkpoint entry. If cars are bumper-to-bumper here, expect 30min+ wait. This is your primary indicator.
📷
#2702 — Woodlands Flyover
Approach road to checkpoint. Queue backing up here = heavy traffic. Clear road = smooth entry.
📷
#2704 — BKE (Woodlands)
BKE expressway feeding into checkpoint. If congested here, traffic has backed up onto the expressway — very heavy.
📷
#9703/9704/9705 — SLE Approach
SLE leading to Woodlands. If these are slow, the jam extends far back. Usually last to congest, first sign of clearing.
